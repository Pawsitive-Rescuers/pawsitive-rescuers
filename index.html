Who we are

Pawsitive Rescuers is a community of Palmetto Bluff residents united by our shared love for rescue animals. Whether you foster, adopt, volunteer, donate, or simply care deeply about animal welfare, this group is for you. We share stories, celebrate rescues, organize support efforts, and lend a helping paw or hoof whenever we can.

Join us in making a difference, one tail at a time.

Share and celebrate

We share stories and celebrate rescues, in our Facebook group and at community events.

Partners

We raise funds and rally neighbors to support local rescues. The Pawsitive Rescuers Fund is administered by the Community Foundation of the Lowcountry, a 501(c)(3) public charity.

Lend a paw or hoof

Foster, adopt, volunteer or donate. There is a place for everyone who cares.

Rescues we support

Visit their websites to meet adoptable animals and learn how to help. We are adding horses to our mission.

Hilton Head Humane Association

Hilton Head Island, SC

A no-kill shelter that takes in stray, abandoned and abused animals from Hilton Head, Bluffton and Daufuskie.

Visit website

Humane Association of the Lowcountry

Beaufort County, SC

An all-volunteer group devoted to homeless cats and kittens. Volunteers manage feral cat colonies and foster cats in their own homes.

Visit website

Lovable Paws Rescue

Ridgeland, SC

A volunteer-run, no-kill dog rescue and sanctuary. They take in sick, injured and homeless dogs, care for them and find them loving homes.

Visit website

One Love Animal Rescue

Savannah, GA area

Helps abandoned, neglected, abused and unwanted dogs and cats by partnering with shelters, rescue groups and the community to place them in loving homes.

Visit website

Renegade Paws Rescue

Savannah, GA

A volunteer-based rescue caring for unwanted, abused and injured dogs. Offers adoption, foster and volunteer programs.

Visit website

Shaduba Home for Life

Ridgeland, SC

Provides permanent, loving homes for dogs in need and micro-fosters rescue dogs while they wait for safe, lasting adoptions.

Visit website

Holiday drive

Keep the animals in the rescues in your heart this holiday season. There are multiple ways you can help.

  • Give to the Pawsitive Rescuers Fund through the Donate button.
  • Buy items from a rescue's Amazon wish list: Hilton Head Humane Association, Lovable Paws Rescue, One Love Animal Rescue and Renegade Paws Rescue. More wish lists coming soon.
  • Give directly to a rescue: Hilton Head Humane Association, Humane Association of the Lowcountry, Lovable Paws Rescue, One Love Animal Rescue, Renegade Paws Rescue or Shaduba Home for Life.
